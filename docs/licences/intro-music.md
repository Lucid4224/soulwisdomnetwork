# Intro and outro music

The soundtrack of `assets/podcast/intro.mp4` (used as the intro and again as the outro) was
generated from scratch with code, in the same script that rendered the video: tones, filtered
noise and synthesized gong and chime sounds. No music library, samples or recordings were used,
so there is no third-party copyright and no licence is needed.

Proof: the AI conversation that made it, `intro-music-origin.png` (recorded 1 October 2026).

If the intro is ever replaced with library or commissioned music, keep that licence here.
